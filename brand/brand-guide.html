Color

Your five colors are all soft mid-tones, so nothing in the set is dark enough for small body text. Plum Ink, a deep shade of Vintage Lavender, is added for text. It keeps the palette's mood and reads clearly (9.6:1 contrast on Linen).

  • Use roughly 60% Linen and Sand, 30% Lavender and Plum, 10% Amethyst and Taupe.
  • Only use Amethyst Smoke and Rosy Taupe for decoration, never for text (they reach only 2–3:1 contrast on Linen).
  • Vintage Lavender works for headings and buttons. For small text, use Plum Ink.
